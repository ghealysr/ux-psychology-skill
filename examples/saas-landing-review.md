# Example: SaaS Landing Page Psychology Review

> Demonstrates how to use the skill to review a page design.

## Prompt
"Review my SaaS landing page through a psychology lens. It has a hero with headline + subheadline + CTA, a features section with 3 columns, a pricing section with 3 tiers, testimonials, and a FAQ."

## Review Output

### Principles Currently at Work

| Principle | Where | Assessment |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| First Impressions | Hero section | ✅ Headline communicates value — but is it specific enough? "Save time" is weaker than "Cut reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes" |
| Anchoring | Pricing section | ⚠️ If all three tiers are shown equally, there's no anchor. Highlight the recommended tier visually |
| Social Proof | Testimonials | ✅ Present — but are they specific? "Great product" is weak. "Saved us $47K in Q3" is strong |
| Chunking | Features section | ⚠️ Three columns of features may overwhelm. Consider prioritizing the ONE feature that matters most |

### Missed Opportunities

| Principle | Where to Apply | Suggestion |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Loss Aversion | Hero or pricing | Frame around what they lose by NOT using the product, not just what they gain |
| Default Effect | Pricing | Pre-select the recommended tier. Most users won't change the default |
| Commitment/Consistency | Above pricing | Add a free tool or calculator BEFORE showing pricing — once they've invested effort, they're primed to subscribe |
| Peak-End Rule | Page bottom | The FAQ section is often the last thing users see. End with a strong testimonial + CTA, not a FAQ |

### Anti-Pattern Warnings

- The three-column feature section risks "feature grid blindness" — consider a staggered or narrative layout instead
- If the CTA says "Get Started" — that's generic. "Start Your Free Trial" or "See It In Action" tells the user what happens next

### Priority Changes (Highest Impact)

1. **Anchor the pricing** — make the middle tier visually dominant
2. **Specificity in hero** — replace "save time" with a specific metric
3. **End with impact** — move the strongest testimonial to the bottom, above the final CTA
